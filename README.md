## About

This entry is a single funny one-liner about fitness trackers and the gap between what our gadgets celebrate and what we actually do with our day. The joke turns a proud milestone notification into a quiet confession, and the punchline lands on the very last word.

The humor works because the setup sounds like a genuine achievement worth celebrating, then the second half reframes every one of those steps as a short trip made for snacks rather than exercise. It is the kind of line that reads even better the second time, once you notice how the watch's praise and the fridge run sit side by side. Running `main.py` writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Usage

```bash
python3 main.py
```
